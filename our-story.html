🏫 ~ 2016 · 6th Standard

The Day I First Saw You

Class 6 mei pehli baar tumhe dekha — aur kuch hua jo samajh nahi aaya uss waqt, par dil ne decide kar liya tha. Wahi se sab shuru hua. 💗

📱💭 Years of Waiting

The Silent Crush Era

Saalon tak tumhe dekh kr heartbeat tez hone dena apna secret talent bana chuka tha. Tumhara online ana aur phone ghoor ghoor kr dekhna ki shayad message karogi 😭 Mera dil sirf tumhara tha — bina thake, bina badle, bina kisi aur ko chance diye.

👫✨ The Best Friend Phase

Best Friends Bane Hum

Tumhari har problem mei sabse pehle available hona, tumhe hasana apna favourite kaam bana lena, aur andar hi andar sochna "kaash yeh bestfriend wala tag thoda upgrade ho jaye" 🙈

📝💍 9 Saal · 9 Promises

9 Sentences & 9 Promises

Kyuki humari kahani ke tab 9 saal ho chuke the — har ek promise aaj bhi utna hi sach hai. "Non-refundable, non-returnable, lifetime warranty type love" — iska warranty period kabhi expire nahi hoga 😌💗

💍🥹 12 August, 2025

The Day I Finally Asked

"Kya tum mere official sukh dukh ki saathi banogi?" — thoda darr tha, dil dhak dhak kr raha tha, haath thode kaap rahe the. Par uss din finally himmat karke bol paya. Itne saalon ka wait, itni saari feelings — sab ek hi din mei reward mil gaya 🥹💗

📱🫂 2025 — 2026

Our First Year — LDR Edition

Screen ke peeche se hug karna, emoji wali kiss bhejna, aur call cutne ke darr se battery percentage dekhte rehna 🥲 Fights bhi dekhi, raatein bhi dekhi jaha sirf silence aur ansu bache the — par humne har baar ek dusre ko choose kiya. Kyuki humari fights bhi humare pyaar se badi nahi ho sakti.

🎉💗♾️ 12 August, 2026

Happy 1st Anniversary! 🎉

Yeh sirf humara pehla saal hai — bhot saare aur aane wale hai. Aur mai har ek ke liye ready hu, tumhare saath, hamesha. Shaadi wale sapne, ghar wale sapne, budhaape wale plans — sab mei tumhara hi chehra dikhta hai 🥹♾️
