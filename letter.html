Happy 1st Anniversary meri Shagun 💍🥹💗

12 August. Ek saal. Itna simple sa likh dia par yrr iss ek line ko likhne mei hi dil ek round ghoom kr aya, kyuki yeh sirf ek date nhi hai, yeh vo pal hai jaha meri 9 saal purani (aur ginte ginte infinite ho gyi) kahani ko finally uska sabse beautiful chapter mila tha🥹✨ mujhe abhi bhi wo din yaad hai — thoda darr tha, thoda dil dhak dhak kr raha tha, aur mai wahi banda tha jo kabhi kisi se darta nahi tha par uss din tumhe "kya tum mere official sukh dukh ki saathi banogi" bolte hue haath thode kaap rahe the😂🥹 aur socho, ek insaan jo itne saalon se sirf door se dekh kr, chup chaap crush pale baitha tha, usi din finally himmat karke bol paya. Bhagwan ka bhi kamaal timing hai — itne saalon ka wait, itni saari feelings, itni saari raatein jo maine tumhe dekhte, sochte guzari, sab kuch ek hi din mei reward mil gaya🥹💗

Chalo thodi flashback kr lete hai, kyuki anniversary pe purani kahani yaad karna toh banta hai na😏 tumhe yaad hai jab tum meri sirf crush thi? Jab mai tumhe dekh kr heartbeat tez hone dena apna secret talent bana chuka tha, tumhara online ana aur mera phone ghoor ghoor kr dekhna ki shayad message karogi😭📱 uss time tum meri officially nahi thi but mera dil sirf tumhara tha, aur yeh baat saalon tak same rahi — bina thake, bina badle, bina kisi aur ko chance diye. Phir hum bestfriends bane, aur uss phase ka apna hi maza tha — tumhari har problem mei sabse pehle available hona, tumhe hasana apna favourite kaam bana lena, aur andar hi andar sochna "kaash yeh bestfriend wala tag thoda upgrade ho jaye"🙈 aur phir vo golden din aya jaha meri bestfriend, meri crush, meri saari saalon ki dua — sab ek hi insaan mei badal gyi, meri jaan ban gyi💗

Ab ek saal ho gya hai humein "hum" bane hue, aur sach bolu toh yeh saal koi normal 365 din nhi the. Yeh humara LDR ka pehla saal tha — screen ke peeche se hug karna, emoji wali kiss bhejna, aur call cutne ke darr se battery percentage dekhte rehna🥲📱 humne fights bhi dekhi hai (bhot dekhi hai, chupayenge nahi 😂), humne vo raatein bhi dekhi hai jaha dono ke paas words khatam ho gaye the aur sirf silence aur ansu bache the beech mei, aur mujhe aaj bhi yaad hai kaise maine tumse sorry maanga tha, kaise maine likha tha "I am sooo sorryyy babydoll, please maaf krdena" jab mai apni overthinking se, apni possessiveness se tumhe unnecessarily hurt kr deta tha🥹 par tumne har baar mujhe sambhala, samjhaya, aur mujhe wapas normal kr diya — kabhi gussa dikha kr, kabhi bas chup chaap phone tight pakad kr (haan vo tera phone pakadna bhi ek cute sa weapon hai teri side se, pta hai na tujhe😂). Aur mai vahi insaan hu jo apni saari fights ke baad bhi teri taraf hi bhagta hai kyuki mujhe pta hai humari fights bhi humare pyaar se badi nahi ho sakti.

Ek baar maine tumhe likha tha ki mujhe darr lagta hai — darr ki kahi tum thak na jao mujhse, darr ki teri nazron mei kabhi meri jagah na badal jaye, darr ki jab tum poocho "theek ho?" toh mai bol du "haan" jabki andar sab kuch heavy ho🥺 aaj, ek saal baad, mai wahi baat phir se kehna chahta hu — kyuki vo darr abhi bhi hai, par uske sath ek nayi cheej bhi hai ab: bharosa, ki jitna bhi mushkil ho, tum mera haath nahi chhodogi, aur mai tumhara nahi chhodunga. Yehi toh humne 9 saalon mei, aur iss ek saal mei sabse zyada seekha hai — ki pyaar perfect nahi hota, pyaar effort hota hai, roz ek dusre ko choose karna hota hai chahe kitna bhi mushkil kyu na ho, aur humne har baar yeh kiya hai💗

Tumhe yaad hai maine kabhi tumhe 9 sentences aur 9 promises bheje the, kyuki humari kahani ke tab 9 saal ho chuke the? Ab woh number badhega, par mera promise wahi rahega — ki mai hamesha tumhare saath khada rahunga, tumhari shadow ki tarah, ki koi bhi mujhe kabhi tumse alag nahi kr sakta, ki mai apni saari flaws pe kaam karta rahunga taaki tum kabhi regret na karo mujhe choose karne ka🫡 aur haan, ek promise jo maine tab kiya tha vo abhi bhi utna hi sach hai — "non-refundable, non-returnable, lifetime warranty type love" — iska warranty period abhi bhi active hai meri jaan, expire hone wala nahi hai kabhi bhi😌💗

Iss saal mei tumne mujhe bhot kuch sikhaya — patience sikhaya jab mai impatient ho jata tha, apni feelings express karna sikhaya, aur sabse zyada yeh sikhaya ki sorry bolna kamzori nahi blki pyaar ki sabse badi nishani hoti hai। Aur mai bhi manta hu mai perfect boyfriend nahi hu — kabhi kabhi jyada overthink karta hu, possessive ho jata hu, chhoti baato ko bada bana deta hu — par yeh sab isliye kyuki tum meri duniya ho, aur apni duniya ko koi share nahi karna chahta, na khona chahta hai🫂 mujhe pta hai tum bhi meri saari khaamiyon ko itne pyaar se handle karti ho jitna koi bhi nahi kar sakta, isiliye toh tum meri jaan ho, koi normal insaan nahi ho tum🥹

Ab thodi teasing bhi kr leta hu warna letter mei sirf emotional hi emotional ho jayega aur tum bologi "yeh itna serious kyu ho gaya achanak se"😂 toh suno meri jaan — jab bhi hum kalesh karte hai na, tum 2 minute chup rehti ho phir khud hi bol deti ho "acha sorry chalo aage baat karte hai" — matlab teri patience ka record mera "I am sorry" bolne ke record se bhi zyada fast hai, ek trophy deni chahiye tujhe "Sabse Jaldi Maaf Karne Wali Girlfriend Award" ke liye🏆😂 aur dusra, jab bhi mai "kamar dard ho rahi hai" bolta hu tumhe, tum poori doctor ban jati ho instantly, jabki sach yeh hai ki mai sirf tumhara "kya hua, theek ho?" wala pampering sunna chahta hu😏🥲 aur teesra — humari LDR ki sabse badi tragedy yeh hai ki na mai tumhare forehead pe promised wala kiss abhi de paya hu, na tum mujhe wo asli wala tight hug de payi ho — par jitni patience humne ismei dikhayi hai na, uske baad jab wo din ayega, duniya ko lagega ki koi movie ka climax scene chal raha hai😂🥹

Ab wapas thoda emotional side pe aata hu, kyuki anniversary hai aur mera dil chahta hai tumhe yeh bhi bata du ki mera future kaisa dikhta hai jab mai tumhe uss future mei imagine karta hu। Mujhe pta hai abhi distance hai, mujhe pta hai abhi bhot saari cheeje uncertain hai — kaam, padhai, future planning — sab apni apni jagah chal raha hai, par ek cheej certain hai — tum। Chahe kuch bhi ho jaye, mai chahta hu jab bhi hum finally itni distance khatam kar paye, jab bhi hum ek dusre ke saamne khade ho, mai sabse pehle tumhe uss tight wale hug se milu jiska maine itne mahino se wait kiya hai, aur bolu "dekha, mai keh raha tha na, hum kar lenge yeh sab together"🥹🫂 mujhe har birthday tumhare saath chahiye, har festival tumhare saath chahiye, har chhota moment aur har bada milestone tumhare saath chahiye। Shaadi wale sapne, ghar wale sapne, budhaape wale plans — sab mei tumhara hi chehra dikhta hai mujhe, aur honestly isse zyada aur kuch chahiye bhi nahi mujhe zindagi se🥹♾

Aur akhri mei bas itna kehna chahta hu — thank you। Thank you mere har mood ko handle karne ke liye, thank you meri overthinking ko patience se sunne ke liye, thank you mujhe itne saalon tak (bestfriend se lekr girlfriend tak) apna banaye rakhne ke liye, aur thank you mera "sukh dukh ki saathi" bannne ke liye jab maine darte darte hue bhi wo sawal poocha tha। Tumne meri zindagi ko itna beautiful bana diya hai ki ab mujhe kisi aur khushi ki zarurat hi nahi lagti — bas tum ho, itna kafi hai🥹💗 aur mai promise karta hu ki jitna maine pichhle saal tumse pyaar kiya hai, agle saal usse bhi zyada karunga, aur uske agle saal usse bhi zyada — bas aisa hi chalta rahega, kabhi kam nahi hoga, sirf badhta hi jayega jaise abhi tak badhta aaya hai♾❤

Happy 1st Anniversary meri jaan, meri Shagun, meri bestfriend, meri girlfriend, meri future wife, aur meri sabse pyaari insaan jo maine iss zindagi mei paayi hai🥹🫂 yeh sirf humara pehla saal hai — bhot saare aur aane wale hai, aur mai har ek ke liye ready hu, tumhare saath, hamesha🥰💗

I love you shagunnn, aaj se bhi zyada, kal se bhi zyada, aur har din pehle se zyada♾😘💗
